#TSJH Preoperative Care Handbook

Preoperative Medical Evaluation & Optimization

A practical clinical reference designed to support consistent, evidence-based preoperative evaluation and medical optimization of adult patients undergoing elective noncardiac surgery, with emphasis on spine and orthopedic surgery.

This handbook brings together perioperative cardiovascular guidance, pulmonary risk assessment, medication management, laboratory and diagnostic interpretation, standardized screening tools, and facility-specific clinical workflows into one accessible resource.

📘 Purpose of This Handbook

The goal of this handbook is to help the preoperative team:

* Standardize preoperative medical evaluations
* Identify patients who require additional optimization before elective surgery
* Recognize findings that warrant additional testing or specialist evaluation
* Reduce unnecessary testing and consultations
* Reduce preventable same-day surgical cancellations
* Improve communication between providers, nursing, anesthesia, surgeons, and ancillary staff
* Support safe and efficient perioperative decision-making
* Apply current evidence-based perioperative recommendations consistently

The preoperative assessment includes:

* Medication reconciliation

* Duke Activity Status Index (DASI)
* STOP-BANG
* Clinical Frailty Scale (CFS)
* Pulmonary risk assessment
* Arozullah Respiratory Failure Risk Index when indicated

🫀 BNP / NT-proBNP Screening

BNP or NT-proBNP testing may be appropriate in patients with increased perioperative cardiovascular risk.

The handbook includes guidance for:

* Determining who should receive biomarker screening
* Interpreting BNP and NT-proBNP results
* Identifying patients who require postoperative ECG surveillance
* Determining when postoperative troponin surveillance should be considered

Facility-specific protocols should always be followed.

✅ Final Preoperative Optimization Status

Each evaluation should result in a clearly documented status:

OPTIMIZED

The patient currently meets applicable perioperative criteria to proceed with the planned elective procedure.

PENDING

Additional information, testing, records, specialist recommendations, or medical optimization is required before final determination.

NOT OPTIMIZED

A clinically significant condition or unresolved risk is present that should be addressed before proceeding with elective surgery.

Avoid ambiguous terminology such as simply stating that a patient is “cleared for surgery.”

⸻

🏥 TSJH Perioperative Workflow

When applicable:

Patient meets TSJH perioperative consensus guidelines to proceed with elective surgery on scheduled surgery date at this time.

Final decisions regarding whether to proceed with surgery remain multidisciplinary and may involve the surgeon, anesthesia team, preoperative medical provider, and appropriate consultants.

⚠️ Clinical Disclaimer

This handbook is intended as a clinical reference and educational resource for healthcare professionals.

It does not replace:

* Independent clinical judgment
* A complete patient-specific medical evaluation
* Anesthesia assessment
* Surgeon decision-making
* Specialist consultation when indicated
* Hospital policy
* Facility protocols
* Current evidence-based clinical guidelines

Recommendations must be individualized according to the patient’s medical history, current clinical condition, planned procedure, anesthesia requirements, and applicable institutional policies.

When facility policy differs from general recommendations presented in this handbook, the applicable institutional policy and current approved clinical protocol should be followed.
