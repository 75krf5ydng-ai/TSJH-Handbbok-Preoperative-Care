Audit Interpretation

Working: The linked handbook page was successfully located.

Missing: The page could not be found or returned an error. Verify the filename and capitalization or create the missing HTML file.

Duplicate: The same destination appears more than once in the handbook index. Some duplicates may be intentional.

External: The link points outside the handbook and is listed separately from internal page integrity scoring.

Anchor: The link points to another section on the same page and does not require a separate HTML file.
