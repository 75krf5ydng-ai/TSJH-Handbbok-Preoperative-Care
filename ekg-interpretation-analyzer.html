3. Preoperative Interpretation

Clinical Safety

Do not use this tool to rule out acute coronary syndrome, unstable arrhythmia, or another emergency. New ischemic changes, symptomatic high-grade conduction disease, unstable rhythm, chest pain, syncope, or other acute findings require direct clinical evaluation.
