Final Preoperative Readiness & 24–48 Hour Master Checklist

Last review before elective surgery: unresolved risks, medication holds, interval changes and final optimization status

Home Provider Workflow RN Workflow Anesthesia Cancellation Prevention Resources

Purpose

The final 24–48 hour review is designed to catch changes that occurred after the initial preoperative evaluation and verify that required testing, medication holds, specialist recommendations and anesthesia plans are actually complete.

A patient should not arrive on the day of surgery with a clinically meaningful issue that was already known but never closed.
This checklist is intentionally concise. Use the detailed specialty chapters when a specific abnormality requires interpretation.

QUESTION 1
Is there any active medical instability?

YES → Not optimized / urgent evaluation as appropriate
NO → continue

QUESTION 2
Is any required test, recommendation, medication plan or anesthesia decision still unresolved?

YES → Pending
NO → continue

QUESTION 3
Does the patient exceed the capabilities of the planned surgical setting?

YES → HLOC / venue review
NO → continue

QUESTION 4
Are all medically relevant issues addressed with an appropriate perioperative and postoperative plan?

YES → Optimized

Pending Documentation Example

FINAL PREOPERATIVE OPTIMIZATION STATUS: Pending. Final determination is pending ___, which is required because ___. Item has been assigned to ___ and will be reviewed upon completion before scheduled surgery.

Not Optimized Documentation Example

FINAL PREOPERATIVE OPTIMIZATION STATUS: Not optimized. Patient currently has ___ requiring treatment/stabilization before elective surgery. Required evaluation/intervention includes ___. Surgical team notified.

Day-of-Surgery Change

Even after final preoperative optimization, a meaningful day-of-surgery change in symptoms, vital signs, medications or clinical status requires reassessment. Optimization reflects the patient's status at the time it is documented.

Key Takeaways

  • The final review should confirm—not assume—that all required items were completed.
  • Ask specifically about interval health changes since the original H&P.
  • Verify exact last doses for medications that materially affect surgery.
  • Close anticoagulant, antiplatelet, SGLT2, GLP-1 and insulin questions before DOS.
  • Required cardiac/device testing must be reviewed, not merely obtained.
  • Confirm postoperative cardiac surveillance when BNP/NT-proBNP triggers are met.
  • Confirm postoperative respiratory planning for OSA and significant pulmonary disease.
  • Do not call asymptomatic bacteriuria an active UTI without symptoms.
  • Every required pending item needs an owner and closure.
  • Pending means something clinically important remains unresolved.
  • Not optimized means a current problem requires treatment, delay or higher-level planning.
  • Optimized means the necessary medical evaluation and perioperative plan are complete.
