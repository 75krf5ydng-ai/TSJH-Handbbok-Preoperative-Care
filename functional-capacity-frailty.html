How to Use This Tool

Complete the self-reported DASI activities and enter the patient's Clinical Frailty Scale score and mobility information. The tool calculates the DASI, estimated peak oxygen consumption and estimated METs, identifies functional or frailty concerns, and generates a patient-specific perioperative summary.

Important: DASI is a self-reported functional-capacity measure. The Clinical Frailty Scale requires clinical judgment and should be based on the patient's usual baseline function rather than temporary disability from an acute illness or isolated orthopedic limitation.
