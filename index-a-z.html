How to Use This Index

Use the alphabetical index to locate clinical topics, calculators, workflows, forms and perioperative decision-support resources. Multiple terms may link to the same chapter when a topic can reasonably be searched by more than one name.

Clinical navigation: This index is designed for rapid point-of-care navigation. TSJH institutional guidance remains the primary source for facility- specific eligibility, escalation and higher-level-of-care pathways.

Final Optimization Status

OPTIMIZED: Applicable perioperative requirements are satisfied and no clinically meaningful unresolved issue remains.

PENDING: A clinically meaningful issue remains unresolved and must be completed or reviewed before final optimization.

NOT OPTIMIZED: An active or unstable issue requires treatment, evaluation or an alternative perioperative plan before routine elective surgery.

Documentation: Avoid the phrase “cleared for surgery.” Document the patient's current optimization status and any remaining perioperative requirements.
