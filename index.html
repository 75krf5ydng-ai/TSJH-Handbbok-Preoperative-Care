Final Optimization Status

Status Meaning
Optimized Required evaluation is complete, clinically important conditions have been addressed and no unresolved medical issue prevents the planned perioperative pathway.
Pending A required result, recommendation, medication decision, anesthesia review or other clinically important item remains unresolved.
Not optimized A current medical condition requires treatment, stabilization, delay or higher-level-of-care planning before elective surgery.
Standard optimized language:

Patient meets TSJH perioperative consensus guidelines to proceed with elective surgery on scheduled surgery date at this time.
Avoid the phrase “cleared for surgery.”

Fastest Workflow

For a routine H&P:

1. Open the Provider Quick-Reference Dashboard.

2. Use the Master H&P template.

3. Open a specialty chapter only when an abnormality requires interpretation.

4. Use the medication guide for anticoagulants, GLP-1, SGLT2 and insulin questions.

5. Complete the Final 24–48 Hour Readiness Checklist before surgery.

6. Document the final status as Optimized, Pending or Not optimized.
