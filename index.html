Welcome

This handbook is designed to support consistent, evidence-informed preoperative evaluation of patients undergoing elective spine, orthopedic, pain-management, and other noncardiac procedures.


Content is organized for rapid clinical use by providers, nurses, anesthesia personnel, surgeons, and other members of the perioperative team.

Quick Reference

  • Confirm procedure, surgeon, and surgery date.
  • Identify active medical or cardiovascular concerns.
  • Assess functional capacity with DASI.
  • Calculate RCRI when indicated.
  • Screen for OSA using STOP-BANG.
  • Assess frailty with the Clinical Frailty Scale.
  • Review medications requiring perioperative adjustment.
  • Address clinically significant abnormal laboratory or diagnostic findings.
  • Document final preoperative optimization status.
