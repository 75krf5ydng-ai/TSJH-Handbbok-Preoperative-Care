Purpose

This template provides a consistent structure for preoperative medical evaluation and optimization while remaining concise enough for routine use in TruBridge.

Use the surgery date as the Date of Service and in the procedure line and Assessment & Plan #1.
Avoid the phrase “cleared for surgery.” Use Optimized, Pending or Not optimized for final preoperative status.

BNP SmartPhrase

BNP screening. Obtain BNP because of age greater than 65 years and RCRI score of 1. If BNP is 92 pg/mL or greater, implement the TSJH postoperative cardiac-surveillance protocol with postoperative EKG and daily troponins for 48–72 hours.

Common A&P Language — OSA

Known OSA. STOP-BANG ___. Patient is compliant with PAP / intolerant of PAP and stopped routine PAP use approximately ___. Recommend PAP anytime asleep when tolerated, RT assistance as needed and continuous pulse oximetry when indicated. NIPPV and/or supplemental oxygen if clinically warranted.

Common A&P Language — Stable CKD

CKD with creatinine ___ and eGFR ___. Renal function is stable / changed compared with baseline ___. Avoid nephrotoxins and NSAIDs when clinically appropriate, maintain perioperative volume status and monitor renal function/electrolytes as indicated.

Common A&P Language — Anemia

Anemia with Hgb ___, prior ___, MCV ___. Pattern is most consistent with ___. No evidence of active bleeding / ___. Expected surgical blood loss is ___. Additional treatment/workup ___ required before surgery.

Common A&P Language — Diabetes

Type ___ diabetes with A1c ___ and current glucose ___. Perioperative medication plan reviewed including ___. SGLT2 hold ___ completed. GLP-1 GI symptom/aspiration-risk review ___. Basal insulin/pump plan established as applicable.

Common A&P Language — Anticoagulation

Chronic anticoagulation with ___ for ___. Last dose ___. Renal function ___. Planned hold interval ___. Bridging is / is not indicated according to applicable perioperative anticoagulation pathway. Postoperative resumption guided by surgeon/medical plan after adequate hemostasis.

Common A&P Language — Abnormal UA Without Symptoms

Abnormal urinalysis reviewed. Patient denies dysuria, urinary frequency/urgency, suprapubic pain, flank pain, fever or chills. No clinical evidence of symptomatic UTI at this time. Abnormal UA alone does not establish infection.

Common A&P Language — LBBB

LBBB identified on EKG. Prior tracing/testing reviewed: ___. Under the TSJH perioperative pathway, adequate prior cardiovascular assessment is required; when not already established, pharmacologic nuclear stress testing such as Lexiscan/regadenoson or cardiology evaluation is required before elective surgery.
