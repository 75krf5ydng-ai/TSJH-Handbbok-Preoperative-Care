Convert PFT Results Into a Preoperative Recommendation

PFT / Clinical Finding Suggested Preoperative Recommendation
Normal PFT or mild stable abnormality with no respiratory symptoms No additional pulmonary testing is generally required solely because of the PFT. Continue baseline respiratory therapy and routine postoperative pulmonary expansion measures.
Stable obstructive disease with FEV1 ≥50% predicted Assess symptoms, CAT/BODE/Arozullah, oxygen use and recent exacerbations. If otherwise clinically stable, proceed using appropriate pulmonary optimization and postoperative monitoring.
FEV1 <50% predicted Higher-risk pulmonary finding under the TSJH pathway. Review CAT, BODE, Arozullah, oxygen requirements and recent exacerbations. Pulmonary and/or anesthesia review may be required to determine surgical venue and postoperative monitoring.
Restrictive pattern with reduced TLC Identify the cause. Review DLCO, imaging, oxygen requirement and symptoms. Pulmonary review is appropriate when restriction is significant, unexplained or associated with impaired gas exchange.
Restriction + markedly reduced DLCO Consider interstitial/parenchymal lung disease. Review pulmonary records, imaging, oxygen requirement and pulmonary hypertension risk before elective surgery.
Obstruction + reduced DLCO Consider emphysema or pulmonary vascular disease. Assess oxygenation, functional status and clinical severity.
24-hour supplemental oxygen Higher-risk finding under the TSJH outpatient pathway. Anesthesia/pulmonary and higher-level-of-care review are appropriate.
Chronic hypercapnic respiratory failure Not a routine outpatient-risk condition. Pulmonary/anesthesia review and higher-level-of-care planning are appropriate.
Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis Higher-risk pulmonary condition. Review current pulmonary status, oxygen use, DLCO, pulmonary hypertension and appropriate surgical venue.
Active COPD or asthma exacerbation Treat the acute exacerbation before routine elective surgery. Final preoperative optimization status should remain Pending or Not optimized depending on severity.
Poor-quality or technically unreliable PFT Do not base major perioperative decisions on unreliable values. Repeat the study or obtain pulmonary review only when the result is necessary to answer a current clinical question.
