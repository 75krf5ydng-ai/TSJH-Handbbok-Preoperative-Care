How to Use This Tool

Enter the available PFT values below. The tool will evaluate for evidence of obstruction, restriction, impaired diffusion and bronchodilator response and then place the findings into a perioperative context.

Whenever the pulmonary laboratory reports a formal lower limit of normal (LLN), z-score or interpretation, that laboratory-specific information should take priority over a fixed percentage cutoff.

This tool is intended for clinical decision support. It does not replace the formal interpretation of a pulmonary function laboratory, pulmonologist, anesthesiologist or institutional perioperative policy.

Interpretation Reference

Finding Interpretation Preoperative Significance
FEV1/FVC below LLN Supports airflow obstruction. Interpret with symptoms, FEV1 severity, exacerbation history and oxygen need.
Reduced FVC with normal/high FEV1/FVC May suggest restriction but does not confirm it. Review TLC. Spirometry alone cannot confirm restrictive lung disease.
TLC below LLN Supports a restrictive ventilatory defect. Identify cause and review DLCO, imaging, symptoms and gas exchange.
Reduced DLCO Indicates impaired diffusing capacity. Consider emphysema, interstitial lung disease, pulmonary vascular disease, anemia and other causes.
Obstruction + reduced DLCO May be compatible with emphysema or combined pulmonary disease. Assess oxygenation, symptoms and clinical severity.
Restriction + reduced DLCO Raises concern for parenchymal/interstitial disease. Review imaging, oxygen requirement and pulmonary hypertension risk.
FEV1 <50% predicted More severe airflow limitation. Higher-risk pulmonary finding in the TSJH outpatient pathway.
24-hour supplemental oxygen Advanced respiratory disease marker. Requires anesthesia/pulmonary and higher-level-of-care consideration.
Chronic hypercapnic respiratory failure Advanced ventilatory impairment. Not a routine outpatient-risk condition; consider higher-acuity surgical setting.
Active COPD / asthma exacerbation Acute pulmonary instability. Treat before routine elective surgery.

Important Interpretation Principles

Do not diagnose restriction from a low FVC alone. A reduced TLC is required to confirm a restrictive ventilatory defect.
Do not rely solely on a fixed FEV1/FVC ratio of 0.70. Use the pulmonary laboratory's lower limit of normal or z-score whenever available.
Bronchodilator response should be interpreted using the testing laboratory's current ATS/ERS methodology and reference equations. This calculator provides a preliminary estimate only.

TSJH Higher-Risk Pulmonary Findings

The following findings warrant additional attention when determining whether the planned outpatient surgical setting is appropriate.
  • FEV1 <50% predicted
  • 24-hour supplemental oxygen
  • Chronic hypercapnic respiratory failure
  • Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis
  • Clinically significant unresolved pulmonary exacerbation
  • Severe functional limitation or other concerning pulmonary risk scores

ATS / ERS Reference

Formal PFT interpretation should use contemporary pulmonary laboratory reference equations, lower limits of normal and ATS/ERS interpretive standards.
