How to Use These Forms

These forms are designed to standardize preoperative information collection and reduce missing data before elective surgery.

The questionnaires support clinical evaluation but do not replace provider judgment, anesthesia assessment or institutional policy.

Key Takeaways

  • Use the patient questionnaire to identify risk before the formal H&P.
  • The RN form standardizes history, medication and testing collection.
  • The provider form supports focused medical interpretation and optimization.
  • The anesthesia form creates a specific reason for review rather than a vague “high risk” referral.
  • High-risk medications should be identified early.
  • Recent hospitalization and interval health change should be screened again before surgery.
  • Forms should support—not replace—clinical judgment.
  • Final status should remain Optimized, Pending or Not optimized.
