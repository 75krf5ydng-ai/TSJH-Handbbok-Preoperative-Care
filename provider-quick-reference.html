Diabetes Medication Pearls

SGLT2

Commonly 3 days for most agents; 4 days for ertugliflozin. Watch for euglycemic ketoacidosis.

GLP-1

Use current anesthesia guidance and assess escalation phase, GI symptoms, gastric-emptying/aspiration risk, and procedure context.

Basal insulin

Do not completely stop reflexively; use the facility dose-adjustment protocol.

Insulin pump

Ensure a clear perioperative pump/glucose-management plan.
