How to Use This Tool

Use only the calculators that apply to the individual patient. A numerical risk score supports clinical judgment but does not replace assessment of current symptoms, oxygen requirement, pulmonary stability, anesthesia risk or appropriateness of the surgical venue.

Clinical Reminder

Pulmonary risk calculators are screening and risk-stratification tools. Abnormal scores should be interpreted in the context of current respiratory symptoms, baseline function, procedure risk, anesthesia plan, pulmonary hypertension, oxygen requirement and surgical venue capabilities.

Do not delay elective surgery solely because a screening score is elevated without determining whether the finding represents an active or modifiable clinical problem.
