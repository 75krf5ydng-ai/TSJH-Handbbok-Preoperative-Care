Preoperative Provider, RN & Anesthesia Questionnaires

Structured intake forms for consistent medical, nursing and anesthesia screening

Home Evaluation Screening Tools SPA Scope Communication

Purpose

Structured questionnaires improve consistency, reduce missed risk factors and help route clinically important information to the correct member of the perioperative team.

These forms are designed to complement—not replace—the provider history, physical examination, medication review and clinical judgment.

High-Risk Trigger Questions

These questions should trigger immediate provider or anesthesia review when answered “yes.”

Question Escalation
New chest pain? Provider/anesthesia immediately
New or worsening dyspnea? Provider/anesthesia
Recent stroke/TIA? Provider + anesthesia timing review
Recent PCI/stent? Provider/cardiology/anesthesia medication review
Anticoagulant hold unclear? Provider/anesthesia/surgeon
New oxygen requirement? Provider/anesthesia
Severe GI symptoms while on GLP-1? Anesthesia review
SGLT2 inhibitor not held? Provider/anesthesia metabolic review
Prior difficult airway? Anesthesia review

Key Takeaways

  • Use separate provider, RN and anesthesia questionnaires because each role captures different risk information.
  • Use structured questions to reduce omissions.
  • Escalate acute symptoms rather than simply documenting them.
  • Medication reconciliation should specifically identify anticoagulants, antiplatelets, SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 agents, insulin and chronic steroids.
  • Ask specifically about prior difficult airway and anesthesia complications.
  • Document PAP use or intolerance in known OSA.
  • Ask about interval ER visits and hospitalizations.
  • Every pending item should have an owner.
  • Use these forms to support—not replace—clinical judgment and the formal preoperative H&P.
